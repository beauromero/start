Add link

Bulk add links

One link per line — bare URLs, [Title](url), “Title — URL”, or an open-tab dump from a copy-tabs extension. A line that isn't a URL starts a group for the links after it (created, or matched by name); when every URL has its own line above it, those lines are read as titles instead. You'll choose and route everything on the next screen.

Bulk edit

Change titles, URLs, icons, and groups across the whole board, then save once. Empty icon = automatic favicon; emoji, 1–2 letters, or a built-in icon token like :star: work. Moved links land at the end of their new group.
